# Initial settings for volume-controlled mechanical ventilation in children

> Markdown transcription of
> `Settings_for_mechanical_ventilation_of_infants_and_children.pdf`.
> The source page is attributed to UpToDate. The original PDF remains the
> authoritative source.

| Setting | Infant (<1 year of age) | Toddler/child (1 to 12 years) | Adolescent (>12 years) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tidal volume (mL)** | 5 to 8 mL/kg (healthy lungs); 3 to 6 mL/kg (lung-protective strategy) | 5 to 8 mL/kg (healthy lungs); 3 to 6 mL/kg (lung-protective strategy) | 5 to 8 mL/kg (healthy lungs); 3 to 6 mL/kg (lung-protective strategy) |
| **Rate (breaths/minute)** | 20 to 30 | 15 to 25 | 12 to 20 |
| **PEEP (cm H₂O)** | 3 to 8 | 3 to 8 | 3 to 8 |
| **Pressure support (cm H₂O)**[^pressure-support] | Minimum 6 to 10 | Minimum 6 to 10 | Minimum 6 to 10 |
| **Peak inspiratory flow (L/minute)** | Adjusted to fit desired inspiratory time | Adjusted to fit desired inspiratory time | Adjusted to fit desired inspiratory time |
| **Inspiratory time (seconds)** — targeted, based upon changes to inspiratory flow | 0.4 to 0.6 | 0.7 to 0.9 | 0.9 to 1.2 |
| **FiO₂ (%)**[^fio2] | Start with 1.0, rapidly wean to ≤0.6 | Start with 1.0, rapidly wean to ≤0.6 | Start with 1.0, rapidly wean to ≤0.6 |
| **Flow trigger (L/minute)** | 0.25 to 0.5 | 0.8 to 2 | 0.8 to 2 |
| **Pressure support cycle** | 10 to 25% of peak flow rate | 10 to 25% of peak flow rate | 10 to 25% of peak flow rate |

## Abbreviations

- **PEEP:** positive end-expiratory pressure.
- **FiO₂:** fraction of inspired oxygen.

## Source notes

Consultation with an expert in the mechanical ventilation of children (for
example, a pediatric intensivist or pediatric anesthesiologist) is strongly
encouraged. Regardless of the ventilator settings employed, the physician must
assess ventilator settings shortly after initiation and frequently thereafter,
and adjust them as needed to meet oxygenation and ventilation goals as the
natural course of the underlying pathophysiology evolves.

[^pressure-support]: Adjust according to endotracheal tube size: 3 to 3.5 mm: 10 cm H₂O; 4 to 4.5 mm: 8 cm H₂O; ≥5 mm: 6 cm H₂O.

[^fio2]: Wean to 0.6 or below to maintain arterial oxygen tension (PaO₂) of 60 to 80 and oxygen saturation (SpO₂) of 92 to 97% when required PEEP is <10 cm H₂O. If required PEEP is ≥10 cm H₂O, targeted oxygen saturation may be reduced to 88 to 92% for patients with pediatric acute respiratory distress syndrome (PARDS).
